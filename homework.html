Firmware Version:

Are you using an ARC or a NARC?

1. LED brightness check. The LED can be distracting for users who prefer a more subdued lighting environment.

Procedure:
- Go into your Sonance app and select low brightness mode to enable this feature.
- Please gauge the brightness of the status LED in 'LOW' brightness mode.

2. Protection LED timing. The protection LED was delayed in previous builds, causing a delay potentially confusing users.

Procedure:
- Use amp in normal mode with audio playback
- Short circuit left and right speaker together
- Record time between audio OFF and protection LED displayed.

3. Mute volume release. Ensure that when the amp is muted, pressing volume up or down unmutes the amp.

Procedure:
- Use amp in normal mode with audio playback.
- Select MUTE option with TV remote.
- Press volume up or down with TV remote.

4. TV mute CEC command stays in mute state after 30 seconds of mute.

Procedure:
- Verify that the TV mute CEC command operates correctly.
- Wait 30 seconds and ensure ARC does not restore output automatically.

5. IR Learning Setup

Procedure: Program remote and test power ON/OFF.

6. Input Switchover Behavior

Procedure: Test dual source switchover.

7. DSP EQ Frequency Adjustment

Procedure: Rapidly adjust EQ and monitor for issues.

8. Factory Reset

Procedure: Reset amp and verify settings.
